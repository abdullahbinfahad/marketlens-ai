How MarketLens works

The domain runs a browser-native free-provider system. Optional credential-backed connectors stay protected in the Streamlit backend and are never placed in this public page.

Five-layer architecture

1. Live Product APIsDummyJSON, Fake Store API, Open Beauty Facts, plus optional marketplace connectors.
2. Integration & normalizationProducts are standardized, deduplicated, and tagged with provider evidence and source URLs.
3. AI analyticsSentiment, trend, risk, confidence, and deterministic explanations run without a paid key. DeepSeek is optional on the protected backend.
4. MarketLens dashboardSearch, comparison, provenance, and profit simulation are available on this domain.
5. Decision supportGO, CAUTION, and PASS are research-prioritization labels, not promises of sales.
